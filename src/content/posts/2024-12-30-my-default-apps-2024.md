- 🔖 **Bookmarks:** [Raindrop.io](https://raindrop.io/)
- 📆 **Calendar:** Apple Calendar
- 💬 **Chat:** Signal, iMessage & WhatsApp[^1]
- ☁️ **Cloud storage:** iCloud & Dropbox & Mega[^2]
- 🧑‍💻 **Code Editor:** [VSCode](https://code.visualstudio.com/)
- 🙍🏻‍♂️ **Contacts:** Apple Contacts
- ⌨️ **Launcher:** [Raycast](https://www.raycast.com/)
- 📮 **Mail Client:** [Spark Mail](https://sparkmailapp.com/)
- 📨 **Mail Service:** ~~[Opalstack](https://opalstack.com/)~~ → [FastMail](https://fastmail.com/)[^3]
- 🎵 **Music:** Spotify
- 📝 **Notes:** Apple Notes / [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/)[^4]
- 🔐 **Password Management:** ~~[Bitwarden](https://bitwarden.com/)~~ → [1Password](https://1password.com/)[^5]
- 🌅 **Photo library:** iCloud
- 🎤 **Podcasts:** Apple Podcasts
- 📑 **Read Later:** ~~Omnivore~~ → [Pocket](https://getpocket.com/)[^6]
- 🗞️ **RSS Client:** ~~Feedly~~ → [FreshRSS](https://freshrss.org)
- 🔎 **Search:** ~~[DuckDuckGo](https://duckduckgo.com/)~~ → [Kagi](https://kagi.com/)[^7]
- 🛒 **Shopping Lists:** Apple Reminders
- ✅ **To-Do:** [Todoist](https://www.todoist.com/)
- 🌐 **Web Browser:** [Arc](https://arc.net/)
- 🕸️ **Web Hosting:** ~~[Opalstack](https://opalstack.com/)~~ → [Uberspace](https://uberspace.de/)

---

[^1]: My dream is to get rid of WhatsApp, to be meta free. Unfortunately, 99% of my contacts use it, so I won't see this happening soon.

[^2]: iCloud for the important things, Dropbox for a bunch of legacy stuff, Mega for the expendable ones.

[^3]: Opalstack gave me years of great service and helped me leave Gmail, but I wanted something more reliable, and Fastmail hit the spot. I especially love the _masked email_ feature and the integration with 1Password.

[^4]: I tried to fully move to Obsidian, but Notes is too convenient.

[^5]: Bitwarden is great, but 1Password is way better. I love the _developer tools_.

[^6]: Unfortunately, [Omnivore was acquired](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/omnivore-joins-elevenlabs) and shut down.

[^7]: I would have never thought that I would end up paying for a search engine, and yet here I am. Definitely the best upgrade of my year. I couldn't do without it now. The integration of the _quick answers_ is fantastic. Try it.
